Before you decide.

Answers to the practical questions that matter when choosing your next office space.

Talk to our team
What is a one person office?

A One Person Office is a private, furnished workspace designed for an individual professional. It gives you a dedicated place to work, take calls and focus without sharing a desk in an open coworking area.

What's included in the office?

Offices are offered with essential furnishings. Available internet, utilities, housekeeping and access to shared amenities depend on the chosen plan and location. Our team can share the exact inclusions before you decide.

Can I customise my workspace?

You can discuss preferred furniture, storage, layout and other practical requirements with our team. Customisation possibilities depend on the selected office and plan.

How do pricing and contract terms work?

Pricing depends on your location, office type, chosen plan and commitment. Speak with our team for a current quotation, inclusions and terms before finalising your office.

Can I visit before deciding?

Yes, you can request an office visit to see suitable available spaces and discuss your needs with the team. Visits are subject to the location's schedule and availability.
