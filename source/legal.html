Privacy notice

Information you provide

The website enquiry forms request contact details such as name and telephone number, optional work email, team size, workspace preferences, location, move-in timeline and any information you choose to enter in requirements. Please avoid including sensitive personal information in free-text fields.

How enquiries are delivered

The website is designed to send enquiries to a configured lead service, or to open WhatsApp with your details ready for you to review and send. Enquiry information is used to respond to your request and discuss workspace options. The lead service, if configured, may receive the page address and submission time alongside your enquiry.

Retention and sharing

Final retention periods, service providers, authorised access, transfer arrangements and deletion procedures must be confirmed by the business. No specific retention or security guarantee is made in this draft. Please contact our team with questions about your information.

Cookies & embedded services

The website includes externally provided resources, including Google Fonts and a Google Maps embed. Accessing these resources can disclose technical information such as your IP address and browser details to the provider. Opening WhatsApp or other third-party links is subject to the relevant provider's privacy terms.

The site's complete cookie, analytics and advertising inventory must be verified against the deployed configuration before a final cookie notice is published. Browser settings allow you to manage cookies and site permissions.

Website terms of use

Use this website to explore workspace information and contact our team. Do not misuse forms, interfere with the website or submit information without permission. Website materials are provided for informational purposes; an enquiry is not a confirmed booking or service contract.

Any service arrangement is subject to the applicable agreed workspace contract and applicable law. Nothing in this notice is intended to limit statutory consumer rights.

Bookings, cancellations & refunds

This website's enquiry flow does not itself collect payment or confirm a workspace reservation. Ask the team to confirm availability, pricing, deposits, notice periods, cancellation conditions and refund eligibility in writing before committing. The applicable signed or agreed service terms and mandatory legal rights determine the specific arrangement.

Approved payment, cancellation and refund schedules must be supplied by the business before this section is finalized.

Offers & workspace information

Workspace options, amenities, photographs, indicative walking times, pricing and promotional offers may vary by location, availability and plan. Confirm the actual workspace and any applicable offer conditions with the team. A promotional enquiry alone does not establish eligibility for a discount.
